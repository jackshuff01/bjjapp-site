Questions

Does it only work for BJJ?

BJJ is the focus, but each person can track several martial arts. Arts with a grading syllabus get belt progress, and arts without belts still work for logging.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Can students sign up on their own?

Not yet. Logins come from your coach or gym admin, so ask them to get in touch if your gym isn't on it.

Do members have to pay online to use it?

No. Members can use the app whether or not they pay online, and coaches can record cash and bank transfers.

What happens to video from the Roll Camera?

Frames are analysed on the app's server and never saved. Separately, coaches can choose to upload gym photos to help train the model. They must confirm that everyone pictured agreed, only they and the app's admins can see those photos, and they can delete them at any time.
