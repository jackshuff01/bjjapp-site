Questions

What does the Gym plan include?

Logins for your coaches and every coaching tool: lesson plans, attendance, belts, syllabus sign-offs and payments. Members need their own login to see their data and use the student features.

What if a member doesn't pay for a login?

They stay on your register. Coaches can still record their attendance, gradings and payments, and it's all there if they sign up later.

How does the free month work?

You get every feature for your first month and don't need a card to start. We'll get in touch before it ends, and you only pay if you want to carry on.

My gym isn't on BJJ Insights. Can I still use it?

Yes. A member login is £5 a month after your free month, whether or not your gym is on BJJ Insights.

Does it only work for BJJ?

BJJ is the focus, but each person can track several martial arts. Arts with a grading syllabus get belt progress, and arts without belts still work for logging.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

How do I sign up?

Email us and we'll set up your login. Gyms get logins for their coaches, and each member gets their own.

Do members have to pay their gym online to use it?

No. Members can use the app however they pay their gym, and coaches can record cash and bank transfers alongside online payments.

What happens to video from the Roll Camera?

Frames are analysed on the app's server and never saved. Separately, coaches can choose to upload gym photos to help train the model. They must confirm that everyone pictured agreed, only they and the app's admins can see those photos, and they can delete them at any time.
